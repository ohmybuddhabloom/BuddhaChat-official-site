Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

BuddhaChat ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the BuddhaChat mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple ID identifier and, optionally, your name and email address (if you choose to share them). We also support anonymous accounts that do not require personal information.

Profile Data: Display name and spiritual preferences you provide during onboarding.

Practice Data: Meditation session records, practice statistics, and merit points generated through your use of the App.

Chat Content: Messages and media you choose to send in community group chats (Dojos), questions you post in the Q&A feed, and prompts you send to Ask. Ask conversations may be retained in app or service history so that you can continue a conversation. Optional long-term Ask memory is used only when you enable it.

Device Information: Push notification tokens for delivering notifications to your device.

Usage and Analytics Data: We collect information about how the App is used to understand engagement and improve BuddhaChat. This includes a randomly generated visitor identifier, your account identifier when you are signed in, basic device details such as operating system, device type, brand, and model, screens viewed, app-open events, sign-in and sign-up events, and referral or campaign attribution included in links you open. This information is stored in our Supabase backend for internal reporting and is not sold or shared for third-party advertising.

Media Data: When you choose to share a photo, file, voice message, or video in Community, the selected media is uploaded for delivery to the conversation. Camera and microphone access is requested only after you choose a feature that needs it.

Voice Input: When you choose voice input in Ask, audio is recorded temporarily and transmitted for speech-to-text processing. The resulting transcription is placed in the Ask input field.

Purchase Information: Apple App Store or Google Play processes and stores payment information for digital purchases. We receive purchase confirmation, product, entitlement, and subscription status through RevenueCat, but not your complete credit card details. For physical product purchases via PayPal, PayPal processes payment; we receive the shipping and order information needed to fulfill the order.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's features (meditation tracking, community chat, AI guidance)
  • To personalize your experience based on your spiritual preferences
  • To send push notifications you have opted into
  • To facilitate community features (Dojo membership and Q&A feeds)
  • To deliver media that you choose to share in Community
  • To measure App usage, diagnose product issues, and understand referral or campaign performance
  • To manage purchases, subscriptions, and premium access
  • To fulfill physical product orders placed through PayPal

3. Device Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Camera and Photo Library: Used only when you choose to take or select media to share in Community.
  • Microphone: Used only when you choose Ask voice input, record a Community voice message, or record a video with audio. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Push Notifications: Used to deliver practice reminders and community updates. You can manage notification preferences in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Authentication and database hosting (data stored in the cloud)
  • Stream — Activity feed infrastructure
  • WuKongIM — Community real-time messaging and conversation history
  • Expo — Push notification delivery
  • AgentChat and Groq — Processing and delivery of AI-powered spiritual guidance
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management and purchase validation
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — Payment processing for digital purchases
  • PayPal — Payment processing for physical product purchases

5. Data Retention

We retain account data while your account is active and for only as long as needed to provide the App, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce our agreements. You can delete your account in the App's Profile settings or submit a request through our Account Deletion page. Deletion removes the account, profile, and analytics events linked to your account identifier from our primary systems. Content already shared with other users in a Community conversation may remain in conversation history in a de-identified form; you may request review of specific content through the Account Deletion page. Aggregated or anonymous analytics that cannot reasonably identify your account may remain. Limited security, fraud-prevention, transaction, or backup records may be retained when legally permitted or required.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), secure token storage provided by iOS and Android through expo-secure-store, and Row-Level Security (RLS) policies on our database to protect your data.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. BuddhaChat prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation and maintains published Child Safety Standards for its Community features.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data through the App
  • Update your profile information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data through the App or our Account Deletion page
  • Opt out of push notifications through your device settings
  • Revoke microphone access through your device settings
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App.

10. Contact Us

For account deletion, use our Account Deletion page. For other privacy questions, contact us at: buddhachat.app@gmail.com
