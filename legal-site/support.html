BuddhaChat Support

Last updated: July 28, 2026

We are here to help with BuddhaChat accounts, subscriptions, purchases, Community features, Ask, meditation practice, and technical issues.

Contact Support

Chat on WhatsApp Email Support

Please do not send passwords, payment card numbers, Apple or Google verification codes, or suspected child sexual abuse material through WhatsApp or email.

Include These Details

  • Your BuddhaChat display name and the sign-in method you use.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • A short description of the issue and the steps that caused it.
  • A screenshot, if it does not contain private or sensitive information.
  • For purchase questions, the store used and the order or transaction reference. Do not send full payment details.

Account and Subscription Help

  • To delete an account, follow the instructions on our Account Deletion page.
  • To cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription, use the subscription settings in the store where it was purchased.
  • For digital purchase restoration, use Restore Purchase in BuddhaChat.

Safety Reports

Use the in-app Report and Block actions for Community content. For urgent child-safety concerns, follow our Child Safety Standards. Do not attach or forward suspected CSAM.
